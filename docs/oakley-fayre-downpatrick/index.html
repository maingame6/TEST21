WHAT WE'D IMPROVE

Too much of the discovery journey sits on third-party listings.

When searching for Oakley Fayre, prominent results are still Tripadvisor and business directories, and the owned domain was not reliably accessible to the crawlers we tested. That doesn't mean customers cannot use the site, but it does suggest there is room to improve technical accessibility and owned-search visibility.

  • Make the owned site easier for search engines to crawl and understand.
  • Give café, deli, bakery/catering and hamper content distinct search-friendly sections.
  • Use local schema, Search Console and Google Business Profile work to strengthen the connection between the website and local searches.
